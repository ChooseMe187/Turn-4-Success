GOOD TO KNOW / 05

Questions before
we get to work?

The right information makes the first conversation more useful.

Ask about your property ↗
Can I request work for multiple units?+

Yes, the request form allows you to specify the number of units and outline the work. Individual scopes and scheduling are confirmed directly.

What should I include for a useful quote?+

Share the property type, city or ZIP code, services needed, target timeline and a clear description. You can include photos of the work.

Can we discuss recurring maintenance?+

Absolutely. Describe the properties and ongoing maintenance needs, and request a conversation about a recurring service arrangement.

Is requesting a quote a confirmed appointment?+

No. A quote inquiry starts a conversation. Work scope, scheduling, availability and pricing require direct confirmation.
